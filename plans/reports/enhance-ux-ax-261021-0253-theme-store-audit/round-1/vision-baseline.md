**2. Rubric (0–3)**
- **First impression: 1.** desktop-home-first.png has a headline and one CTA, but the CTA is grey. tablet-home-first.png and mobile-home-first.png show no headline or CTA.
- **Brand recall: 2.** Bone, black and oxblood, square media and editorial photography hold together (desktop-home-full.png, desktop-stores-full.png), and add-to-cart is crimson (desktop-product-first.png). The plain "Devs2 Rebel" text wordmark and the grey hero CTA weaken it.
- **Content punch: 2.** The tailoring copy is specific ("The tailored anti-uniform", "Quiet pieces for loud lives"). Against it: "Example product title", the merchant-instruction text on FAQ, cut-off comments, and "This is a gift card for the store" (desktop-product-gift-card-full.png).
- **Clarity and hierarchy: 2.** The product page has one crimson primary and an outline secondary (desktop-product-first.png). Collection pages put Best Sellers ahead of the filters (desktop-collection-all-first.png), and title weights are mixed.
- **Storytelling: 2.** The home page runs category, product, lookbook, craft, atelier, reviews, journal, press (desktop-home-full.png), and the About timeline and team are strong (desktop-about-full.png). The placeholder product and the empty journal break the flow.
- **Knowledge and trust: 1.** No shipping or returns information on the product page (desktop-product-full.png) or in the footer, which has a single link. The gift card shows sold out and comments are cut off. Positives: "In stock", the 24h reply time and hours (desktop-contact-full.png), and open/closed state on stores (desktop-stores-full.png).
- **Motion: not scorable.** Stills only, so easing, hover and drawer motion can't be judged. What I could see: the hero autoplays to a different slide between first and loaded (diff 29.7% desktop, 35.3% tablet, 68.9% mobile), and the desktop CTA looks half faded in at first paint.
- **Responsive: 2.** Grids step cleanly from 4 to 3 to 2 columns (desktop/tablet/mobile-collection-all-full.png). Against it: one-card carousels at 375, the mobile buy box below the fold, broken table headers on mobile, and the tablet blog promo.
- **Accessibility (visual only): 2.** Most text over photos measures at least 4.5:1 at the median. Failures: the slide 3 eyebrow (3.73–4.06:1), text over the coat on mobile, and the disabled gift-card pills (about 1.5:1). Focus rings can't be seen in stills.
- **Performance feel: 2.** On most routes first vs loaded differs only by the 1px header band or not at all. The tablet collection page shifts about 30px when the carousel dots arrive, and the mobile and tablet hero is not ready at first paint.

**3. Anti-AI-look / retail rubric (0–3)**
- **Corner radius: 3.** Buttons, inputs, cards and media are square, and pills and badges are rounded as DESIGN.md specifies (desktop-product-first.png). The only miss is the disabled gift-card pills turning square.
- **Type: 2.** One Inter family throughout with uppercase tracked eyebrows ("12 COLLECTIONS"). Against it: the bold vs regular H1 split, the one-off "AS FEATURED IN" style, and small card meta text on desktop.
- **Icons: 2.** Header, card and filter icons share a consistent thin line. The share row mixes filled and outline glyphs.
- **Color: 2.** The palette is disciplined, with crimson on add-to-cart, sale prices and the cart count (desktop-collection-all-full.png). Off-system: the black Subscribe on the blog, white bands on About and the blog, and the grey hero CTA.
- **Density: 2.** Desktop 4-column and mobile 2-column grids are good. Against it: one-card carousels on mobile, Best Sellers duplicating the grid, the 1720px before/after image, and the 970px placeholder block.
- **Copy: 2.** Strong tailoring vocabulary. Against it: placeholder and instruction copy, "Devs2 Admin", duplicate article titles, and machine-sounding blog titles ("Bio-Textiles: Mycelium, Algae, and the Lab-Grown Revolution").
