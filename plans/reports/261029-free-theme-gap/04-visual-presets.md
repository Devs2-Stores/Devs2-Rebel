# Gap slice 04: visual design, home storytelling, preset design systems (2026-10-29)

Rebel (`E:/Work/shopify`, one preset "Rebel") compared with Horizon 4.3.0 presets Fabric, Savor, Vessel, Dwell, Tinker and Pitch. This is read-only evidence: no theme file was edited. All scripts and captures are under `scratch/gap-visual/` (gitignored), and screenshots are in `scratch/gap-visual/shots/`.

## 1. Scope and method

**Scripts**
- `presets.cjs` and `presets-effective.cjs` compute the preset values resolved against schema defaults and palette references. Output: `presets-out.txt` and `presets-effective-out.txt`.
- `home-compose.cjs` lists template section order and blocks. Output: `compose-index.txt` and `compose-index-blocks.txt`.
- `schema-overlap.cjs` measures global setting-id overlap with Dawn 15.4.1 and Horizon. Output: `schema-overlap-out.txt`.
- `capture.cjs` is headless Playwright Chromium. It took full-page and fold captures and measured computed styles, sections, headings, CTAs, hover and focus. Output: `metrics-*.json`, summarised by `summarize.cjs` into `summary-home-1440.txt`, `summary-home-375.txt` and `summary-about.txt`.

**Captured**

- Product card before and after hover at 1440.
- Rebel was captured from `http://127.0.0.1:9292/` and `/pages/about`. Horizon was captured from the public demo stores; the about routes are `/pages/our-story`, `/about-us`, `/about-our-brand` and `/our-story-2`.

### 2.1 Theme Store design-review risks (distinctiveness and preset quality)

1. **Originality of the editor** (gap 2). The storefront is visibly Rebel, but the settings model is 55 % Dawn ids, in Dawn's order. This is the highest risk under the "derived from Dawn" rule.
2. **First impression versus Horizon.** All six Horizon presets open with a signature device: a 184 px wordmark over video (Fabric), an 88 px serif display (Vessel), a jumbo wordmark (Pitch), or a colour-blocked hero (Savor, Pitch). Rebel's first screen is a dark slideshow photo with a 48 px centred line under a solid header (`strip-rebel-home-1440-a.jpg`, `sheet-horizon-home-1440.jpg`). The palette (bone, crimson, oxblood) and the photography are distinctive. The type and layout scale are not (gaps 3-5).
3. **Preset install quality.** There is a dead global setting (gap 1), a 0 px featured section (gap 8) and a 1975 px before/after (gap 9). A reviewer can see all three in five minutes.
4. **Single preset.** The four additional presets are a deferred phase item (not counted).

## 3. Rebel equal-or-better (do not rebuild)

- **Storytelling section breadth.** Rebel ships dedicated sections that Horizon 4.3.0 lacks entirely: testimonials, parallax image, layered images, animated counter, timeline, team, a countdown hero (`sections/hero.liquid:740` `end_date`), press logo list, promotion banner and trust badges. `rg -il "countdown|parallax|testimonial"` over `_reference/fabric/{sections,blocks,snippets,assets}` returns nothing. Rebel also has lookbook hotspots (`banner-hotspots`) and before/after as full sections.
- **Body readability.** Rebel body text is Inter 16 px/24 px; all six Horizon presets ship 14 px/19.6 px (`summary-home-1440.txt:127` against `:2`; `presets-effective-out.txt:5`).
- **Focus visibility.** Rebel uses a 2 px solid outline with a 2 px offset; Horizon uses 1 px (`summary-home-1440.txt:150` against `:23,38,84`).
- **CTA ergonomics.** Rebel CTAs are 191x48 and 140x48 px at both widths. Horizon hero CTAs are text links 20 px tall: Fabric 64x20 at 375, Savor 103x20, Dwell 78x20 (`summary-home-375.txt:113` against `:4,27,56`).
- **Moving media control.** The Rebel slideshow exposes a pause/rotation button (`sections/slideshow.liquid:42`). Horizon hero background video renders with `controls: false` (`_reference/fabric/sections/hero.liquid:173-175`). This last point is code-derived and was not exercised live.
- **Scheme contrast engineering.** `snippets/head-color-schemes.liquid:5-14` mixes the accent, status and subtext colours toward the scheme text colour until they reach 4.6:1. That is at least equal to Horizon's palette lightest/darkest logic (`_reference/fabric/snippets/color-palette.liquid:7-24`).
- **Card hover richness.** On hover Rebel swaps to the second image, zooms (263→268 px) and reveals quick view and add-to-cart (`summary-home-1440.txt:147-149`; `rebel-home-1440-card-hover.jpg`). That is equal to Horizon's zoom, scale and lift effects.
- **Component shape range.** Radius 0-40 plus border and shadow for buttons, pills, inputs, cards, media, popups and drawers covers Horizon's radius range. Do not grow it further: it is part of the Dawn-shaped risk in gap 2.
- **Art direction coherence.** The editorial photography is consistent (concrete interiors, oxblood and crimson garments) and the crimson accent discipline holds (`strip-rebel-home-1440-a.jpg`, `strip-rebel-home-1440-b.jpg`).

## 4. Open questions

1. Should Rebel adopt `color_palette`, or keep schemes and add only section overrides (gap 10)? Shopify recommends palettes for new themes but does not require them.
2. Does a transparent home header (gap 5) fit DESIGN.md's measured-overlay rule, given the 107 px header currently carries an announcement row as well?
3. Should the home template be trimmed (gap 13) at the cost of fewer showcased sections, with the rest moved to secondary templates or alternate home templates?
4. Which demo product should fill the featured section (gap 8), or should the blank state render placeholders on the live storefront?
5. What is the remediation scope for the Dawn-shaped settings (gap 2)? This depends on slice 01's plan.

**Not verified in this slice:** the 768 px viewport; live Theme Editor behaviour; whether the `controls: false` on Horizon's hero video leaves no pause control anywhere; Rebel hero CTA colour contrast on the slide photo.
